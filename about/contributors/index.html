Contributors

People recognized in the x64base and LabTalk work.

Primary Author

Derald R Grimwood Jr. is the originator and primary author of the x64base, DotTalk++, DotScript, and Laboratory Campus / LabTalk concept line. His background includes SAP R/3 ABAP/4, HR/Payroll and Finance systems, database conversion, payroll and business software, U.S. Army finance service, and part-time computer instruction at Lane Community College. The project preserves the xBase lineage while using modern C++ implementation work, self-documenting contracts, proof-labeled documentation, and an education-first laboratory model.

See Developer Profile for the project-relevant professional background behind the current development work.

Contributors

Nathaniel "Nate" Grimwood, IT Director / Director of Managed Services is recognized as a human contributor who provided AI-related advice and guidance to Derald R Grimwood Jr. during the LabTalk/x64base development work.

Nathaniel A. Strickland, a student at the South Dakota School of Mines & Technology, is recognized for contributing to the Ollama server and GPTbase. Those contributions informed the isolated local-inference and project-knowledge advisor roles documented in the AI-facing architecture. See Contributor Credits for the associated project record.

The contributor model for this project treats AI-assisted development as part of the engineering record. Human contributors may advise on AI use, source organization, implementation planning, documentation, website publication, and how source contracts connect to public-facing explanations. Human review remains the authority for release decisions, authorship, licensing, and name use.

Contribution Record

LabTalk is being developed as a living laboratory campus. Contributions are expected to leave a trail through one or more of these surfaces:

  • Source code and build scripts.
  • @dottalk.usage, @dottalk.contract, and related SelfDoc contracts.
  • Case stories and runtime proofs.
  • Website pages generated from the vertical documentation system.
  • Lab exercises, education features, and GUI/portal experiments.

This page is a recognition surface, not a substitute for source control history, license notices, or third-party acknowledgements.
