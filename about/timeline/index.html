Timeline

Key milestones from 1993 to 2026.

1993 — The beginning

  • Early xBase tooling and teaching materials
  • First experiments with inspectable file formats

2000s — Practical iteration

  • Field-tested workflows
  • Refinement of indexing and memo handling concepts

2010s — Language & tooling

  • Scripting approaches aimed at education and repeatable data work
  • CLI-first utilities for inspection and maintenance

2025 — Documented AI-assisted development

  • July 21: earliest probable AI-assisted DotTalk modernization activity, inferred from eight coordinated *_modern source artifacts produced in a 44-second batch. The artifacts do not identify an AI model.
  • August 2: earliest direct preserved project-specific AI material, followed minutes later by the earliest explicitly Gemini-attributed project record.
  • December 13: a preserved DotTalk++ archive and message provide later corroboration of the project identity and direction.
  • Read the complete evidence record.

2026 — Canonical unification

  • DBF_64 and FPT64 formalized

  • Documentation, licensing, curriculum, and tooling consolidated under a single platform

  • SelfDoc and the Master Documentation Organizer become part of the system architecture: documentation, proof, and artifact-protection lanes created to keep AI-assisted work reviewable and prevent damage to source, HELP data, generated manuals, diagrams, and staged publication artifacts.

  • The public project-history convention records August 2, 2025 as the earliest directly documented AI-assisted DotTalk date while preserving July 21 as probable.
