<h2 id="interpretation-rules-and-limitations"><a class="no-underline" href="#interpretation-rules-and-limitations">Interpretation rules and limitations</a></h2>
<ol>
<li>ZIP timestamps preserve local wall-clock time with two-second precision,
but they do not encode a timezone. The dates are more reliable than the
timezone attribution.</li>
<li>File timestamps can survive copying or be changed. The mailbox evidence
supplies an independent "existed no later than" bound, not cryptographic
authentication of each internal timestamp.</li>
<li>The July 21 batch strongly suggests automation or AI assistance but does
not identify a model or prove the mechanism.</li>
<li><code>new 1.txt</code> is direct AI-style project output, but its model is unnamed.
The later <code>gemini.txt</code> filename provides explicit model attribution.</li>
<li>This is the earliest evidence found in the reviewed material. An older
archive, chat export, cloud revision, or message could move the documented
date earlier.</li>
</ol>
