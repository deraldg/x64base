==============================================================================
3. THE THING THE VOCABULARY HAS NO WORD FOR
==============================================================================

. descend
Order: DESCENDING.

. smartlist
   187 50000186 Lee                  Taylor
   185 50000184 Williams             Taylor
   164 50000163 Smith                Taylor
   ...
    18 50000017 Williams             Sophia

Same tag, opposite direction. The engine's order state at the CLI is
THREE-valued and DIRECTIONAL -- NATURAL, ASCEND, DESCENDING -- and `DESCEND` is
a verb that changes it without changing the tag.

Contract 4e closes `Order` to TWO values, `physical | ordered`. `ordered` maps
onto ASCEND. There is no way for a UIDEF document to say ORDERED, DESCENDING.
