<h2 id="matrix-authority-website-design-and-retention"><a class="no-underline" href="#matrix-authority-website-design-and-retention">Matrix Authority: Website Design and Retention</a></h2>
<p>This matrix is not only a page-classification table. It is the authority of record
for two website policies: <strong>design</strong> (how pages are structured and what each must
carry) and <strong>retention</strong> (what must be preserved and how). A design or retention
decision that is not reflected here is not yet policy. Both route through the same
entry/closeout gate as any other website change: read the matrix before the pass,
re-audit it at signoff.</p>
<h3 id="design-authority"><a class="no-underline" href="#design-authority">Design authority</a></h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Page structure follows maintenance class.</strong> The class decides how a page may be
edited and what it must expose: <code>maintained_current</code> carries a present-state
region with <code>as_of_date</code>, evidence state, and next gate; <code>generated</code> / <code>derived</code>
regions are produced by their authority and never hand-shaped.</li>
<li><strong>Evidence before ornament.</strong> Every page must answer the Proofed Site Checklist
below. Design may frame and organize evidence; it must not manufacture technical
truth or hide the proof level.</li>
<li><strong>Diagrams.</strong> Conceptual/architectural diagrams are hand-authored <code>maintained</code> and
kept in sync; data-carrying diagrams are <code>generated</code> from the harvest (AIF-032).
Source and generated image are kept together.</li>
<li><strong>Naming and routes.</strong> Visible titles, nav labels, and prose use <code>DotTalk++</code>;
short route buckets may stay stable for continuity but are not the public brand.</li>
<li>New page types, hero/diagram placement conventions, and nav/section changes are
design decisions and must be classified and recorded here when adopted.</li>
<li><strong>Image assets must be license-clean and watermark-free before publish.</strong> A
website-owned image is only publishable with a clear license and no stock
watermark. <strong>Open for the next push (v5):</strong> <code>public/images/talk-family/arctic-fox.png</code>
(the Arctic mascot) may carry a faint stock watermark -- verify its license and
replace it with a clean copy at the same path before or during the v5 flush.
Also open for v5: <code>public/images/story/x64base-glass-engine.png</code> (origin-story
figure) has embedded number plaques that conflict and read as noise -- photoshop
or Firefly them out (content-aware / generative remove; the in-environment image
tools cannot do object removal), keeping the same path.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="retention-authority"><a class="no-underline" href="#retention-authority">Retention authority</a></h3>
<p>The website preserves history rather than overwriting it. The retention rule for a
page or artifact is set by its class and by these gates:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong><code>maintained_current</code> -- preserve event history.</strong> The present-state region is
replaceable, but prior events and dates are retained, not erased.</li>
<li><strong><code>reported</code> -- append-only.</strong> Measurements and checkpoints are appended with
pinned provenance; a new run adds evidence, it does not replace the baseline.</li>
<li><strong>README / orientation artifacts -- versioned.</strong> Preserve the prior version before
replacing it; README history is project provenance.</li>
<li><strong>Historical source lineage -- byte-preserved.</strong> SHA-256-bound, read-only, private
roots omitted; checksums and truth-level labels retained.</li>
<li><strong>Diagram sources retained with images.</strong> Never discard the <code>.mmd</code> / <code>.drawio</code>
source when publishing a generated image.</li>
<li><strong>Publication checkpoints -- pinned.</strong> Accepted manual/reference snapshots and the
commits that served them are recorded and kept recoverable.</li>
</ul>
<p>Retention is enforced at closeout: the matrix re-audit confirms that nothing which
should have been preserved was overwritten during the pass.</p>
