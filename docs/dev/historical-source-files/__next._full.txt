<h2 id="interpretation-boundary"><a class="no-underline" href="#interpretation-boundary">Interpretation boundary</a></h2>
<ul>
<li><code>xbase/</code>, <code>xdll/</code>, and <code>xbase2/</code> are verified siblings in the archive.</li>
<li><code>DOTTALK.C</code> is physically contained in the xBase2 branch.</li>
<li>XDLL is DLL-export-oriented source; no historical <code>xbase.dll</code> binary was
found in the reviewed archive.</li>
