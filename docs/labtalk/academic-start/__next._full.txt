<h2 id="a-30-minute-evaluation-path"><a class="no-underline" href="#a-30-minute-evaluation-path">A 30-Minute Evaluation Path</a></h2>
<ol>
<li>Read the <a href="/docs/labtalk/academic-positioning">Academic Positioning and Research Agenda</a>
to understand the candidate research contribution and its claim boundary.</li>
<li>Review the guided exemplar,
<a href="/docs/labtalk/lesson-records-fields-tables">Records, Fields, and Tables</a>,
to see how a concept, command surface, dataset, observation, and proof record
become a lesson.</li>
<li>Inspect the <a href="/docs/labtalk/runtime-evidence">Runtime Evidence Gallery</a> to
distinguish implemented system behavior from educational hypotheses.</li>
<li>Review the <a href="/docs/labtalk/academic-positioning#evidence-and-research-safeguards">evidence and research safeguards</a>
before proposing classroom evaluation or human-subjects research.</li>
<li>Use <a href="/docs/labtalk/suggest-a-lesson">Suggest a Lesson</a> to offer curriculum
review, a dataset, a lesson idea, or a research question.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="central-academic-question"><a class="no-underline" href="#central-academic-question">Central Academic Question</a></h2>
<p>The Laboratory Campus asks whether students can learn database systems more
deeply when commands, data, runtime state, documentation, provenance, and proof
remain inspectable in one environment.</p>
<p>The current site presents that proposition as a research direction. It does not
claim measured learning gains, established novelty, a completed curriculum, or
formal institutional adoption.</p>
<h2 id="what-can-be-evaluated-now"><a class="no-underline" href="#what-can-be-evaluated-now">What Can Be Evaluated Now</a></h2>
<ul>
<li>the x64base and DotTalk++ runtime surfaces documented by source and runtime
evidence;</li>
<li>the proof-aware lesson model;</li>
<li>the separation of implementation evidence from learning evidence;</li>
<li>the suitability of the guided lesson structure for introductory database
education;</li>
<li>the proposed research questions, safeguards, and collaboration boundaries.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="what-still-needs-academic-work"><a class="no-underline" href="#what-still-needs-academic-work">What Still Needs Academic Work</a></h2>
<ul>
<li>a reviewed primary-source bibliography;</li>
<li>instructor review of lesson sequencing and assessment;</li>
<li>accessibility and prerequisite review;</li>
<li>controlled classroom or usability studies;</li>
<li>appropriate consent and institutional review for human-subjects research;</li>
<li>publication of limitations, failed hypotheses, and negative results.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="collaboration-paths"><a class="no-underline" href="#collaboration-paths">Collaboration Paths</a></h2>
<p>The project is seeking critique, not endorsement by implication. Useful review
can come from database educators, computing education researchers, learning
scientists, HCI researchers, programming-language researchers, technical
writing educators, students, and general-education specialists.</p>
