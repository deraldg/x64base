# Manual Safety Rules

1. Do not promote a claim above its evidence class.
2. Do not silently repair contradictions.
3. Do not use old manual drafts as current truth.
4. Do not describe report-only metadata as loaded metadata.
5. Do not describe design intent as implemented behavior.
6. Do not publish command syntax until HELP/CMDHELP/runtime evidence supports it.
7. Do not let Student, User, and Developer manuals invent separate vocabularies.
