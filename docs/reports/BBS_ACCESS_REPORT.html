Security invariants in force
  • Server binds 127.0.0.1 only. Nothing is reachable from the network.
  • The token is the trust boundary -- Argon2id (libsodium), CSPRNG-minted, verified constant-time.
  • Agents (role.ai_partner) never hold source.mutate or host.network.egress. They read, propose, and post; they do not change source or reach the network.
  • member.derald is the only owner-class identity and the sole committer.
  • member.guest may post to the guestbook and nothing else -- it cannot even read.
