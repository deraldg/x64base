## Color palette (concept)

- Midnight Blue (background)
- Electric Teal (brand accent)
- Orange / Green / Purple / Red (secondary accents)

## Typography

- **Inter** for body text
- **JetBrains Mono** for code and technical labels

## Iconography

- flat
- minimal
- monospace-inspired

> **Status: concept notes, not the implemented identity.** The identity that
> actually ships is the site you are reading -- its tokens, its light and dark
> palettes, and the `check-opacity-scale` gate that keeps every opacity modifier
> on one scale. This page has not been reconciled with it and is kept as the
> original direction rather than presented as a specification.
