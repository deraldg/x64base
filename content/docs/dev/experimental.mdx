This page used to hold an inventory of DotTalk++ experiments and research
directions: the hierarchical data service, Dewey-style hierarchy indexing, and
the Dewey Decimal education surface.

**That material has moved to private development.**

## Why

The inventory described work that exists in source but is not proven: prototype
interfaces, benchmark material without recorded datasets, and an education
surface that is an empty placeholder by contract. Published on a public
documentation site, that reads as a product claim. It is not one yet.

Rather than delete it or leave it published, it moved to a working surface that
is not part of the public site. The work continues; it simply is not making a
claim while it does.

## What this is not

This is not a retraction. Nothing on the old page was withdrawn as false, and
none of the underlying source was removed. The judgement was about **where** the
material belongs, not whether it was true.

It is also not a permanent decision. Anything that earns a validated
implementation, a recorded benchmark, or a defined educational objective can
come back as a normal documentation page.
