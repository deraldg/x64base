The engine is the base of everything on this site: a C++20 runtime that
opens, indexes, relates, validates, and scripts DBF-family data -- classic
x32, Visual FoxPro, and the project's own 64-bit x64 format -- under the
DotTalk++ command shell. It is active beta, stated plainly, and its habit is
to prove behavior with regressions and oracles rather than describe it.

This page is the map. Each panel below is one aspect of the engine and links
to the page that covers it completely -- the detail lives there, not here.

## Reading the status language

One convention makes every page above trustworthy: claims carry tiers.
**Runtime-proven** means a transcript or regression shows it.
**Source-evidenced** means the mechanism is confirmed in source but the
end-to-end run has not happened. **Chartered** means designed and registered,
not built. Absent means absent. Complete is not bragging when it is true --
and the tier system is what keeps it true.
