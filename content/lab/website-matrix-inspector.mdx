This is a **local-only inspection desk** for the website documentation matrix.
It does not copy or replace the matrix. The maintained authority remains the
[Website Documentation Matrix](/docs/dev/website-documentation-matrix).

Use this page during the website phase of a full-stack documentation push and
whenever a product, command, schema, diagram, FAQ answer, status item, or
developer lane changes.

## Inspection order

1. Identify every website surface affected by the upstream change, including
   nearby overview, FAQ, news/current-status, download, and command-reference
   pages.
2. Find the route in the matrix, or confirm that an explicit wildcard row owns
   it.
3. Confirm its maintenance class: `static`, `maintained`,
   `maintained_current`, `derived`, `generated`, or `reported`.
4. Follow the named source and feed direction. Never repair a generated or
   derived page by editing the projection when its authority is elsewhere.
5. Check date semantics. A current-status date, evidence observation date,
   document-run date, and publication date are different facts.
6. Regenerate every affected derived artifact, then inspect the result rather
   than trusting generator success alone.
7. Inspect child routes and adjacent pages. A single fresh page does not prove
   that the subject is fresh across the site.
8. Run the freshness, public-content, route, and build checks appropriate to
   the changed class.
9. Confirm that local-only routes are present in a local build and absent from
   the publish build.
10. Record one disposition for each inspected surface: updated, current with no
    change, deferred with reason, or blocked with owner action.

## What to look for

| Question | Inspection target | Failure signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is every route owned? | Matrix route rows and wildcard rows | An unclassified page or overlapping owners |
| Is the page fresh? | `as_of_date`, current-state region, evidence date, and next gate | Old present-tense claims or an unexplained stale date |
| Is the authority clear? | Source, runtime proof, HELP, metadata, SelfDoc, manual, or website-owned copy | The website becoming an accidental source of truth |
| Is the update direction correct? | Feed and direction gates | Hand-editing generated output or copying website prose upstream |
| Is the evidence honest? | Evidence tier and linked proof | Runtime claims supported only by source or a screenshot without provenance |
| Is the public boundary intact? | Local build, publish build, and local-only strip gate | `/lab` or other private material surviving publication |
| Was the whole subject swept? | Overview, feature page, FAQ, news, reference, downloads, and diagrams | A cherry-picked update surrounded by stale siblings |

## Full-stack website gate

The matrix check is both an entry gate and a closeout gate. At entry, classify
the surfaces before editing. At closeout, re-audit the rendered site and update
the matrix only after the owner signs off the website revision.
