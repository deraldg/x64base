<p>Private, unlisted working reference. Not indexed, not linked from public navigation.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>The AI Portal persists <strong>12 SYS* DBF tables</strong> across two catalogs. Conventions: physical field and
table names are 10 chars or fewer; 64-bit ids and epoch timestamps are stored as <code>N(20,0)</code> decimal
text with <code>0</code> meaning unset; enums persist as small <code>N</code> codes and booleans as <code>L</code>; and <code>VFROM</code> /
<code>VTHRU</code> / <code>ROWVER</code> form a bi-temporal validity window plus an optimistic-concurrency stamp.</p>
