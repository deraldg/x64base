2:Taa2,<h2 id="overview"><a class="no-underline" href="#overview">Overview</a></h2>
<p>RelTalk names the relation-facing workflow in the DotTalk++ / x64base system.</p>
<p>Its subject is connected data across work areas: declared relation edges,
asymmetric same-field/cross-owner links, saved workspace relation state, and
rooted traversal through <code>ERSATZ</code>.</p>
<p>The local project includes same-field relation support and asymmetric relation forms such as:</p>
<pre><code class="language-text">REL ADD SYSCMD SYSSUBCMD ON CAN_NAME TO PARENT
REL ADD SYSSUBCMD SYSENTVAR ON QUAL_NAME TO HELP_OWNR
</code></pre>
<p>Current relation-facing surfaces include:</p>
<ul>
<li><code>REL ADD</code></li>
<li><code>REL LIST ALL</code></li>
<li><code>REL JOIN [DISTINCT|ALL] [LIMIT &#x3C;n>] [&#x3C;child> ...] TUPLE &#x3C;alias>.&#x3C;field>[, ...]</code></li>
<li><code>REL ENUM [LIMIT &#x3C;n>] [&#x3C;child> ...] TUPLE &#x3C;alias>.&#x3C;field>[, ...]</code></li>
<li><code>REL REFRESH</code></li>
<li><code>REL SCANLIMIT [&#x3C;n>]</code></li>
<li><code>WORKSPACE SAVE</code> / <code>WORKSPACE LOAD</code></li>
<li><code>ERSATZ</code>, <code>ERSATZ PATH</code>, and <code>RBROWSE</code></li>
<li>workspace graph and GUI contract docs</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="the-join"><a class="no-underline" href="#the-join">The join</a></h2>
<p><code>REL JOIN</code> walks a declared parent and its children and emits one tuple per
combination, projecting the <code>&#x3C;alias>.&#x3C;field></code> list named after <code>TUPLE</code>. It is a true
enumerator: it delegates to the same walker as <code>REL ENUM</code>
(<code>src/cli/join_engine.cpp</code>). The historical single-row form -- one tuple built
from wherever the relation pointers currently sit -- is preserved as
<code>REL JOIN ONE</code>, and <code>ONE</code> given a child chain is <strong>refused rather than
silently ignored</strong>, because a traversal that never happened should not report
success (AIF-147).</p>
<p><code>REL SCANLIMIT</code> sets the relation engine's per-hop record budget. It caps what a
traversal <strong>finds</strong>, not what is displayed -- lowering it changes match counts
and drops join rows, where <code>ERSATZ LIMIT</code> only shortens the screen. A truncated
<code>REL JOIN</code> or <code>REL ENUM</code> restates the limit and the row count at the result, and
<code>REL LIST</code> says it is reporting lower bounds, because a truncated count is not a
short list: it is a wrong number in the shape of a right one.</p>
